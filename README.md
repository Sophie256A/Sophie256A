##  Featured Projects

**1. Hotel Sales Performance Analysis 2024 | Power BI**
*   Analyzed $46.8M in revenue across 6K customers and 52K nights booked.
*   Built interactive KPIs: Revenue, ADR ($905.3), Discounts, Nights Booked.
*   Uncovered insights: Studio rooms drove $21.1M, SC meal plan 66% of revenue, Corporate is top segment.
*   Recommendations: The hotels should focus on increasing demand during low-performing periods through strengthening corporate partnerships with companies, improving upselling of meal packages, optimizing room pricing and encouraging repeat/direct bookings as these will increase the revenue generated from each customer and booking by 25%.
*   **Tools:** Power BI, DAX (SUMX, CALCULATE), Power Query

**2. SA Food Delivery Sales Dashboard
Analyzed $1.9M in revenue across 5K customers, 5K Total Orders.
Built interactive KPIs: Revenue, Total Orders, Customer, Average Order Value.
Uncovered insights: Market concentration risk in City A. There is drop in revenue on Day 15(mid month). Payments will affected in case Digital payments fail and therefore lose sale. They should use of other payment modes. .
Recommendations: For City D: Launch targeted promotions and add more restaurant partners to increase order volume. For Meal Package Upselling: With an AOV of $37.48, introduce combo packages to push AOV to $45. Bundle top restaurants with high-margin items. For Payment: Promote digital payment discounts to reduce COD operational costs since 43% already use it. For Inventory: Partner closely with RES_0001 and RES_0004 to ensure availability during peak days. Revenue will grow by 30%
Tools: Power BI, DAX (SUMX, CALCULATE), Power Query
Repo: SA Food Delivery Sales Dashboard https://github.com/Sophie256A/Food-Delivery-Sales-Analysis/blob/main/Food%20Delivery%20Screenshot.png
